Hi, I’m Elvin Garaev, a Product Designer originally from Azerbaijan.

When I was five, my family moved to Kharkiv, Ukraine, where I spent most of my childhood and school years. I later studied International Economics and Business at V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University, where I spent six years building a strong foundation in economics and business.

Before design, sports were a huge part of my life. I competed professionally in both judo and football, which taught me discipline, consistency, and how to work toward long-term goals.

I’ve been working in digital and product design for over five years. Today, I focus mainly on FinTech, Banking, Web3, and AI products. With my background in economics and business, I’ve always been especially interested in products connected to finance, where design has to work together with complex business logic and real user needs.

Outside of work, I enjoy an active lifestyle, playing football, going to the gym, traveling, and reading books about marketing and economics.
