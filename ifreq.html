IFREQ FootballFrom a test day to a pro club trial

IFREQ runs test days for young footballers aged 6 to 21 in Belgium, France and with partner clubs across Europe. Players sign up in the app, take tests for shooting, passing and mental strength, and get a player card with a rating, just like in FIFA. Scouts from top academies see the cards and the videos and invite the best players to trials. I led design for the app, the scouting dashboard and the website, and a team of designers, marketers and developers.

01The problem

One sign up for four very different people

The app treated everyone who downloaded it the same way. But that person could be a coach getting ready to run a test day, a 17 year old with their own bank card, a 10 year old who can't pay for anything, or a parent paying for their child.

Families ended up with two separate accounts, one for the parent and one for the child, even though for IFREQ they were one customer. The person who paid and the person who played were not connected.

And then there was the trip. Test days happen in a few cities, while players come from all over Europe. For many families the real price was getting there, not the test itself.

The customer was never one person.

For IFREQ a customer can be a family, a coach or a 17 year old paying for themselves. Each of them needs a different first step, and the old sign up gave everyone the same one. That became the brief: ask who you are first, then show only what that person needs.

04Getting there

The trip was the real price, so we priced the trip

Many players lived far from the nearest test day and had to travel from other cities and countries. Families sorted out food, hotels and flights on their own, and for many that decided whether they came at all. So I designed three packages and the option to pay in parts.

Standard

The test day
  • Entry to the test day
  • Shooting, passing and mental strength tests
  • Player card with rating and video

Plus

05Test day

From the pitch to a player card

On the day, coaches run every test from the app. Players take tests for shooting, passing and mental strength, every attempt is filmed, and the results become a player card with a rating, just like in FIFA. That card is what scouts see.

06For scouts

Every tested player, ready to compare

The other side of IFREQ is a dashboard for coaches and scouts from top academies. They open a player's card, watch the test videos, compare scores side by side and invite the best players to trials.

The new scouting CRM brought player profiles, evaluations, events and video tools together. Scout productivity grew by 35%, and AI tagging cut the time spent on reports in half.

10Reflection

What I learned

In products for kids, the parent is part of the experience even when the child holds the phone. The family account and the travel packages mattered more than any screen polish.

If I started again, I would treat the whole family journey as one funnel from day one, from the first ad to the trial invitation, and measure it together with marketing and sales.
